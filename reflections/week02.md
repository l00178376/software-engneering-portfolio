# Task One:
## Process Model Selection Worksheet

| Project | Process Model | Requirement Stability | Risk / Safety | Timeline | Customer Involvement | Justification | Risk of Chosen Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A – Aegis Flight-Control Software** | **Waterfall** | Requirements are very stable and clearly defined before development starts. | Extremely high risk because a defect could endanger lives. Full testing and documentation are required. | Long 12–18 month certification timeline, so speed is less important. | Mainly formal reviews and approvals rather than constant changes. | Waterfall is suitable because requirements are stable and the project needs clear stages, documentation, testing and traceability. | If requirements unexpectedly change later, going back to an earlier stage can be slow and expensive. |
| **B – Riverside Bakery Website** | **RAD** | Requirements are uncertain and likely to change, especially design and wording. | Low risk because there are no safety or major regulatory requirements. | Very short – 3 weeks, so rapid development is important. | The owner can provide feedback almost every day and wants to see working versions. | RAD is suitable because it focuses on quickly creating prototypes and getting customer feedback. | The fast approach could lead to poor documentation or rushed development if too much emphasis is placed on speed. |
| **C – CampusCircle App** | **Incremental** | Requirements are expected to change significantly after users test early versions. | Moderate risk. There is no major safety risk, but user accounts and data need basic protection. | The team wants a usable product within one month and needs to launch before the next academic term. | Very high involvement through real users, feedback and behaviour after each release. | Incremental is suitable because the team can build the app in small working parts, release an early version, collect feedback and add or change features in later increments. | The final system may become inconsistent or difficult to maintain if frequent changes are made without good planning and architecture. |
