PitchPerfect — Personal Retrospective
What went well?

Prioritising the backlog helped me focus on the most important features first. The Kanban board made it easier to track progress and understand the workflow. The WIP limit of two encouraged me to focus on finishing work before starting more stories.

What didn't go well?

Some stories were more complex than expected, particularly booking a pitch. It was challenging to estimate how much work could be completed during the limited simulation time.

One action for next time

Next time, I will break larger stories into smaller tasks, estimate the effort more carefully, and identify blockers earlier.

Reflection on the Sprint Goal

The sprint goal helped me focus on the main booking journey: finding an available pitch, making a booking, and receiving confirmation.
